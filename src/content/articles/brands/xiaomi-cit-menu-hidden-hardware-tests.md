Xiaomi documents CIT, also called hardware detection, in a touchscreen troubleshooting page. That is a narrow source: it shows how to open CIT for that task, not every test available on every Xiaomi, Redmi, or POCO device.

## Open CIT for the documented touch test

Open the stock Phone app and enter `*#*#6484#*#*` or `*#*#64663#*#*`. You do not need to press the call button.

Xiaomi documents these codes in the context of a drifting or unresponsive touchscreen. If neither code works, check the official support instructions for your exact model and software version instead of trying malformed or undocumented variants.

## Run the touchscreen check

In CIT, open the touchscreen test. Follow the on-screen grid or drawing instructions and check whether every required area responds.

A gap can show that one area did not register during the test. It does not by itself identify the cause. A screen protector, moisture, damage, a replacement display, charging interference, software, or the touch hardware can require separate investigation.

## Scope and limits

The available CIT menu and its labels can vary by model, region, MIUI or HyperOS version, and firmware. The cited Xiaomi page supports the two entry codes and touchscreen testing only. It does not establish a universal list of camera, sensor, audio, fingerprint, radio, battery, or charging tests.

CIT is a point-in-time check, not a health history or repair diagnosis. If the touchscreen test is unavailable or reports a failure, use Xiaomi support or a qualified repair service for the exact device. Exit with Back or restart the phone when finished.
