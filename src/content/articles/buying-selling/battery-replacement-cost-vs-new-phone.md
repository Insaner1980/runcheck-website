Battery replacement prices are not universal. They change by exact model, country, tax, labor, repair route, part availability, and whether resealing or another damaged part is included. Get a current written quote for your exact device before comparing repair with replacement.

## Start with the phone, not a generic price range

Check the battery diagnosis first. On supported Pixel models, [Google explains the Battery health status and when replacement is recommended](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/15738128?hl=en-GB). Samsung and other manufacturers use their own diagnostic and service routes, and their labels vary by model and region.

Then ask an authorized repairer and, if appropriate, a reputable independent repairer for itemized quotes. Confirm whether the quote includes the battery, labor, tax, adhesive or resealing, and any diagnostic fee. A price from another phone or another country is not evidence of what your repair will cost.

## When replacement is the stronger choice

Battery replacement is usually worth considering when the phone still meets your needs, receives security updates, has no major display or board fault, and the repairer can supply a suitable part with a clear warranty. This is editorial decision guidance, not a universal price rule.

The EU's [smartphone ecodesign rules](https://commission.europa.eu/energy-climate-change-environment/standards-tools-and-labels/products-labelling-rules-and-requirements/energy-label-and-ecodesign/smartphones-and-tablets_en) improve parts, repair-information, battery-durability, and software-support requirements for products in scope. They do not establish a retail battery-replacement price.

## When another phone may make more sense

Compare the exact repair quote with the cost and condition of a realistic replacement device. Also weigh remaining software support, display and port condition, storage, camera needs, and whether earlier liquid or impact damage makes the repair uncertain.

Do not use a fixed percentage or a hard currency threshold as a universal rule. The right decision depends on the actual quote and the useful life you expect after repair.

## Before authorizing repair

Back up important data, ask whether water resistance will be retested or restored, confirm the part and labor warranty, and record the phone's condition. If the estimate changes after inspection, ask for approval before extra work begins.
