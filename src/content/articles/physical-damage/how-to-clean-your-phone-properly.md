There is no single cleaning recipe for every phone finish, coating, opening, and seal. Use the instructions for the exact device. The manufacturer examples below stay separate because a product approved for one device is not automatically approved for another.

## Before cleaning

Power the phone off, unplug cables, and remove the case when the device maker instructs you to do so. Keep liquids away from openings and never immerse a phone merely because it has a water-resistance rating.

## Google Pixel guidance

Google's Pixel cleaning page recommends a soft, lint-free cloth and distinguishes routine cleaning from disinfecting. It warns against getting moisture into openings and against harsh or abrasive cleaning. Its wipe and alcohol guidance applies to the Pixel models covered by that page.

Follow the page for your exact Pixel before choosing a liquid or wipe. Do not turn Pixel-specific approval into a rule for every Android phone.

## Samsung Galaxy guidance

Samsung's Galaxy instructions cover exterior glass, ceramic, and metal surfaces. They call for a soft, lint-free cloth and describe distilled water or alcohol-based disinfectant such as 70% isopropyl or ethyl alcohol for the supported surfaces.

Samsung also warns against excess moisture, openings, immersion, compressed air, and bleach. These are Galaxy instructions; check the exact model's manual when its materials or condition differ.

## Apple iPhone guidance

Apple permits specified disinfecting wipes on supported iPhone exterior surfaces, including 70% isopropyl alcohol and 75% ethyl alcohol wipes. Apple warns against bleach, hydrogen peroxide, moisture in openings, immersion, and compressed air, and notes that cleaning products and abrasion can affect the oleophobic coating.

That guidance is for iPhone. It is included as a named manufacturer example, not as proof of a universal Android cleaning chemical.

## Ports, speakers, and cases

Do not insert a toothpick, metal tool, brush, or liquid into a port or speaker unless the exact device maker tells you to use that method. If debris affects charging or audio and the official instructions do not provide a safe user procedure, a qualified service is the safer route.

Case materials also differ. Clean a removed case according to its maker's instructions and let it dry fully before reinstalling it. This article does not rank case materials or assign one chemical to every case.

## Practical rule

Start with the least invasive method allowed for the exact model, usually a clean lint-free cloth. Add moisture or disinfectant only when the manufacturer permits it for that surface. Keep manufacturer scopes separate, and stop if the phone is damaged, open, or recently repaired.
