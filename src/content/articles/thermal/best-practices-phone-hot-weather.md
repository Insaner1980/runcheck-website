Hot weather does not create one universal temperature rule for every phone. The supported range and protective behavior depend on the model, battery condition, workload, charging system, and where temperature is measured.

## What cell-level evidence says

[The Edge et al. degradation review](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2021/cp/d1cp00359c) identifies temperature, state of charge, and load profile as interacting stress factors. Higher temperature can accelerate SEI growth and other mechanisms associated with capacity fade, power fade, and impedance increase. The review does not prescribe a phone case, charger speed, or consumer cooling routine.

## A named Pixel example

[Google's Pixel 7 and Pixel 7 Pro safety guide](https://support.google.com/product-documentation/answer/12164747?hl=en) says those models are designed to work best from 0 to 35 °C ambient temperature. Outside their normal internal range, they may reduce performance or connectivity, stop charging, or power down. This is specific to the named Pixel models and is not a universal phone range.

## Respond to the phone, not a generic threshold

Use the operating and charging limits for the exact model. If it shows a temperature warning, pauses charging, reduces performance, or powers down, stop the demanding activity and follow the manufacturer's instructions. Keep it out of the external heat source and wait until it returns to its documented range before charging again.

This article does not present removing a case, changing charger speed, using a refrigerator or freezer, applying water, or following a fixed cooling time as universally evidence-based procedures. Cases, charging controls, thermal design, and warnings differ by device.

## Long-term perspective

Repeated or prolonged heat exposure can contribute to cell ageing. A single warm session does not by itself establish damage, and no exact lifetime loss can be inferred from a surface reading. Track repeated warnings or charging interruptions and use the manufacturer's diagnostics or service channel if they continue.
